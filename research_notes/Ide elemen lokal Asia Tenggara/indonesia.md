# Indonesian cultural elements for Voice Gift (beyond tumpeng, Dangdut, Toa)

Scope note: 15 web searches (Nov 2026), mostly Indonesian media (detik, Kompas/NU Online, IDN Times, Mojok, GNFI, CNN Indonesia), UNESCO, government culture sites and local university journals. Symbolic meanings of traditions vary by region and source; treat them as popular readings, not doctrine. App mappings (scene / item / finale / mood / tune / sticker / backdrop) are my inferences and are labelled as such.

## 1. Life-event traditions with a "one item per guest" metaphor (birthday, wisuda, weddings incl. regional, baby)

### Takeaway
Several Indonesian rituals are already "many small things given by many people": seserahan trays, Sundanese saweran (coins/rice/candies thrown over the couple), Batak ulos given to the couple, Betawi roti buaya pairs, graduation bouquets (incl. money/snack bouquets), and the Javanese baby ritual tedak siten (7 coloured jadah steps, a cage full of "future" objects). Best new scene candidates: **Seserahan trays (wedding)**, **Saweran shower (wedding finale)**, **Buket wisuda variants (graduation)**, **Tedak siten (baby)**.

### Cited Findings
**Weddings (general / Javanese)**
- Seserahan = gifts brought by the groom's side as a symbol of responsibility toward the bride's family; each item carries a meaning (e.g. a bag = ability to provide for the household) — [Fimela](https://www.fimela.com/lifestyle/read/3839915/filosofi-dibalik-isi-seserahan-pernikahan-ii); detik lists 20 Javanese seserahan items, "from clothing to food" — [detikJateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/budaya/d-6702221/20-seserahan-adat-jawa-dan-maknanya-mulai-pakaian-hingga-makanan/amp); procedure of *srah-srahan* — [detikJateng](https://www.detik.com/jateng/budaya/d-8260798/tata-cara-srah-srahan-manten-tradisi-seserahan-dalam-pernikahan-adat-jawa/amp)
- Kembar mayang: a *paired* decoration of flowers and leaves woven from janur (young coconut leaf); read as advice for the couple / end of single life; meaning varies, and even within the kraton it is described as just a symbol of a maiden-and-bachelor marriage — [Popbela](https://www.popbela.com/relationship/married/filosofi-makna-dan-aturan-kembar-mayang-dalam-pernikahan-adat-jawa-00-925lr-r7tfx1); [Orami](https://www.orami.co.id/magazine/kembar-mayang); [Tirto](https://tirto.id/mitos-fakta-kembar-mayang-pengantin-maknanya-dalam-pernikahan-gMMQ)

**Sundanese**
- Saweran happens right after the akad nikah; coins = wealth/material sufficiency, rice and turmeric (yellow rice) = enough food/prosperity, candies = sweetness (or the sweet-and-bitter of married life), flowers = future happiness; items are *siloka* (symbols) — [Orami](https://www.orami.co.id/magazine/pernikahan-adat-sunda); [Gramedia](https://www.gramedia.com/literasi/upacara-adat-sunda/); [Unisba journal](https://ejournal.unisba.ac.id/index.php/mediator/article/download/1225/777); accompanied by *tembang sawer* (sung verses) — [NU Online](https://www.nu.or.id/opini/tembang-sawer-sunda-dan-nilai-nilai-islam-LXROl)
- Criticism: children scrambling for coins/candies can get hurt — [Mojok](https://mojok.co/terminal/saweran-tradisi-pernikahan-sunda-yang-sebaiknya-dihilangkan/)

**Betawi**
- Roti buaya (crocodile bread) is brought by the groom as a symbol of loyalty; usually a pair (bigger male, smaller female); traditionally hard and for display, now soft and shared with guests (some historians call sharing it a misunderstanding) — [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/gaya-hidup/20150622111040-262-61510/roti-buaya-sejatinya-simbol-kesetiaan); [Jakarta Culture Office](https://dinaskebudayaan.jakarta.go.id/news_web/detailnews/the-philosophy-of-crocodile-bread-in-a-marriage-tradition-of-betawi-people); [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2017/01/19/inilah-filosofi-buaya-dalam-adat-pernikahan-betawi); [indonesia.travel](https://www.indonesia.travel/gb-en/travel-ideas/gastronomy/roti-buaya/)
- Betawi seserahan list — [Medcom](https://www.medcom.id/gaya/family/3NOoDe7N-6-seserahan-adat-betawi)

**Batak Toba**
- Mangulosi = giving ulos (handwoven cloth) to the couple, conveying warmth/blessing/affection; usually by the higher-status party (e.g. tulang / the bride's side), accompanied by gondang music; some couples and some churches skip it — [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2023/11/04/tradisi-mangulosi-pada-pernikahan-batak-toba); [GNFI 2024](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2024/01/03/mangulosi-tradisi-batak-yang-terus-dilestarikan-bukan-sekadar-kain-biasa); [JOM Unri](https://jom.unri.ac.id/index.php/JOMFSIP/article/download/7063/6748)

**Minangkabau**
- Suntiang: the bride's large gold/silver headdress, tiers in odd numbers (7–11), motifs drawn from nature ("alam takambang jadi guru"); its weight = a woman's responsibilities after marriage; also worn at *malam bainai* — [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/indonesiana/nasional/filosofi-suntiang-dalam-pernikahan-tradisional-adat-minang); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suntiang); [Hipwee on bainai](https://www.hipwee.com/wedding/prosesi-malam-bainai/)

**Balinese (religious, not wedding)**
- Penjor: tall curved bamboo pole decorated with janur and harvest produce, put up for Galungan as thanks for prosperity; janur read as a naga's skin; taken down and burned after Galungan–Kuningan — [detikBali](https://www.detik.com/bali/budaya/d-7213376/makna-penjor-yang-dipasang-saat-hari-raya-galungan-di-rumah-rumah-warga-bali/amp); [RRI](https://rri.co.id/denpasar/budaya/2553542/mengapa-penjor-dicabut-setelah-galungan-ternyata-ada-makna-mendalam); [Tirto](https://tirto.id/makna-penjor-galungan-sebagai-simbol-naga-dan-cara-membuatnya-gNuR)

**Graduation (wisuda)**
- Wisuda is "identical with" flower bouquets (roses most popular); often with a teddy bear in toga; money bouquets (folded notes as petals), snack bouquets, doll bouquets, even cigarette bouquets have become alternatives — [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/life/inspiration/5-tips-memberi-hadiah-buket-saat-wisuda-jangan-asal-pilih-01-jdjm4-mj6vpx); [Mojok](https://mojok.co/terminal/bosen-hadiah-wisuda-boneka-dan-bunga-terus-ini-lho-solusinya/); [IDN Times snack bouquet](https://www.idntimes.com/life/diy/buket-snack-00-mm7zv-rwlhjd/amp); Mojok jokes an *es teh manis* is a better gift than a bouquet — [Mojok](https://mojok.co/terminal/buket-bunga-hadiah-wisuda-paling-gak-guna-mending-es-teh/amp/)

**Baby (Javanese tedak siten)**
- Held at 7 *lapan* (7×35 = 245 days, ~8 months), the baby's first touch of the ground: walks over **7 coloured jadah** (sticky-rice cakes), climbs a **sugarcane ladder** (tangga tebu), scratches in sand (ceker-ceker), then sits in a decorated **chicken cage (kurungan)** holding objects (pen, money, jewelry, tools); what the baby grabs hints at its future; sometimes udhik-udhik coins scattered, flower bath, tumpeng — [detikJogja](https://www.detik.com/jogja/budaya/d-7200701/upacara-tedak-siten-pengertian-urutan-dan-maknanya/amp); [Kemendikdasmen WBTB](https://referensi.data.kemendikdasmen.go.id/budayakita/wbtb/objek/AA001306); [Hipwee](https://www.hipwee.com/young-mom/makna-tedhak-siten/)

### Inferences
- **Wedding alt scene "Seserahan"**: each voice = one decorated tray (hantaran) with a dome lid: fabric, shoes, cake, fruit, roti buaya, sirih, Al-Qur'an-free generic items (see sensitivity). Finale: lids lift all together. Very SVG-friendly (circle tray + dome + bow).
- **Wedding finale "Saweran"**: a shower of coins, yellow rice, flower petals and candies falling onto the couple's names — a natural "throw" finale for the existing lanterns scene or a Sundanese variant. Cheerful, non-religious.
- **Betawi wedding decoration/topper**: a pair of roti buaya on the table (topper option or sticker). Pair → maps neatly to the couple.
- **Batak wedding**: each voice = an ulos cloth draped on a line; finale "mangulosi" (cloths wrap the couple's names). Pattern = simple red/black/white stripes, easy SVG. Medium recognisability outside Batak.
- **Minang**: suntiang as a decoration/backdrop (gold fan shape behind the name); tiers could grow with voice count (7, 9, 11…). Pelaminan (layered gold-red dais) as backdrop.
- **Kembar mayang**: twin janur ornaments framing the wedding scene (decoration, two sides).
- **Graduation variants**: bouquet `SHAPES`/wrap options "Money bouquet" (each flower = a folded banknote — draw generic colored notes, not real Rupiah designs), "Snack bouquet" (each voice = a chip bag/wafer/chocolate), teddy bear in toga as sticker/topper. High recognisability for 18–35.
- **New occasion "Baby / Tedak siten"**: each voice = one jadah step in 7 rainbow colours (repeat colours past 7), finale: the baby's foot steps up the sugarcane ladder into the cage and "picks" a future (random object: laptop, mic, stethoscope, guitar). Also usable for a generic "new baby" gift. Aqiqah not researched (gap).
- Penjor is a Hindu religious offering: OK as a respectful Bali backdrop, not as a joke or a clickable "item".

### Gaps
- No source found on what *guests* give at a Batak wedding (vs family ulos).
- Ondel-ondel and palang pintu (Betawi) not covered by the searches; widely known Jakarta icons (giant puppet pair) but uncited here.
- Aqiqah (Islamic newborn sacrifice) not researched; animal-sacrifice imagery is probably unsuitable for a playful scene anyway.
- No source on "kue tart vs nasi kuning" usage for birthdays among young people.

## 2. Other celebrations that could become occasions (Lebaran, 17 Agustus, Imlek, Natal, farewell)

### Takeaway
Lebaran (ketupat, THR envelope, maaf-maafan) and 17 Agustus (lomba: krupuk race, panjat pinang, balap karung) are the two strongest national, cross-generational candidates; Imlek has been a full national holiday since 2003 and is easy to draw (lanterns, angpao).

### Cited Findings
- Lebaran symbols: **ketupat** linked to Javanese *ngaku lepat* (admitting mistakes), white inside = purity after forgiving; santan = *pangapunten*; **mudik** = going home to reconnect; **THR** from employers, and parents/relatives give money to children (salam tempel) as sharing rezeki; **new clothes** = new spirit — [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/ramadan/read/3981923/6-tradisi-hari-raya-idul-fitri-di-indonesia-selalu-dirindukan); [Kumparan](https://kumparan.com/kumparanstyle/sekelumit-sejarah-tiap-tradisi-idul-fitri); [Kontan MomsMoney](https://momsmoney.kontan.co.id/news/berbagi-thr-mudik-ini-lho-6-tradisi-unik-lebaran-idul-fitri-di-indonesia); [detikHikmah](https://www.detik.com/hikmah/foto/d-6685186/10-tradisi-khas-lebaran-di-indonesia); attribution of ketupat to Sunan Kalijaga appears in popular sources but is not well verified.
- 17 Agustus lomba became popular around the 1950s; panjat pinang adapted from colonial-era entertainment; balap karung origin disputed (Japanese occupation sacks vs Dutch); makan kerupuk read as wartime food scarcity (Kemendikbud via NU) but a historian says krupuk was elite food — [detikJatim](https://www.detik.com/jatim/berita/d-8056997/makna-dan-sejarah-lomba-17-agustus-panjat-pinang-hingga-tarik-tambang); [Bisnis](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20250811/79/1900682/sejarah-dan-makna-lomba-17-agustus-dari-balap-karung-hingga-makan-kerupuk); [NU Online](https://nu.or.id/nasional/berbagai-makna-dan-filosofi-lomba-lomba-hut-ri-duqXa); [JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/nasional/2108150062/lomba-agustusan-warisan-budaya-yang-mempersatukan-bangsa); [detik list of 47 lomba](https://www.detik.com/sumbagsel/berita/d-8048210/47-lomba17-agustus-yang-seru-penuh-semangat-untuk-semua-kalangan-usia)
- Imlek: Gus Dur revoked Inpres 14/1967 (Keppres 6/2000); optional holiday 2001; full national holiday from 2003 (Keppres 19/2002, Megawati). Cap Go Meh (15th day) is not a national holiday; associated with decorated lanterns — [detikEdu](https://www.detik.com/edu/detikpedia/d-7183740/sejarah-singkat-imlek-dari-era-sukarno-sampai-ditetapkan-jadi-hari-libur-nasional); [NU Online](https://nu.or.id/warta/gus-dur-yang-jadikan-imlek-hari-libur-nasional-MzsLb); [detikBali](https://www.detik.com/bali/berita/d-7764317/kapan-cap-go-meh-2025-apakah-libur)

### Inferences
- **Occasion "Lebaran"**: scene = a hanging cluster of ketupat (woven diamond, very easy SVG); each voice = one ketupat; finale "Open the ketupat" (they split to white rice) or "Salam tempel": colourful THR envelopes fly to the recipient. Fits the message theme "mohon maaf lahir dan batin". Recipient phrasing: "Minal aidin wal faizin". Decorations: bedug (drum) and mosque silhouette are recognisable but religious → backdrop only, no jokes. Keep ketupat out of tumpeng overlap (ketupat already a tumpeng dish — fine to reuse drawing).
- **Occasion "17 Agustus / Agustusan"** (or a birthday "Lomba" variant): each voice = a krupuk hanging on a string (krupuk race line); finale "Krupuk race" (all krupuk bitten) or "Panjat pinang" (prizes at the top of a greased pole drop). Red-white flags/umbul-umbul as backdrop. Very recognisable, playful, secular. Sensitivity: treat the Merah Putih flag respectfully (no defacing, no upside-down; UU 24/2009 governs flag use — uncited here).
- **Occasion "Imlek"**: each voice = a red lantern (reuse lanterns scene with a red palette) or an angpao; finale: lanterns glow / angpao rain. Use "Gong Xi Fa Cai" / "Selamat Tahun Baru Imlek".
- Natal, farewell/pindahan, mudik as an occasion: not researched in depth; mudik could be a "send-off" scene (each voice = a car/motorbike in the convoy) — speculative.

### Gaps
- No sources gathered on Indonesian Christmas specifics (e.g. Batak/Manado/NTT customs) or on farewell ("pindahan", "perpisahan") customs.
- Flag-law details not fetched.

## 3. Music and sounds for moods or tunes

### Takeaway
Safe, very recognisable sound sources: angklung (UNESCO 2010), gamelan (UNESCO 2021), street-vendor sounds (bakso bowl "ting-ting", nasi goreng "tek-tek", sate bell, odong-odong songs), kentongan. The well-known Indonesian birthday songs are copyrighted (Jamrud 2002, Adikarso 1961); "Happy Birthday to You" melody is public domain in most places but the Indonesian lyric's author is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- Angklung inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List in 2010 — [UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/00393); gamelan inscribed December 2021 — [NU Online](https://www.nu.or.id/nasional/unesco-tetapkan-gamelan-jadi-warisan-budaya-dunia-tak-benda-kEU8K)
- Street vendor sounds: bakso = spoon on bowl "ting-ting-ting" (Jakarta Post: "the hills are alive with sound noodles"); nasi goreng tek-tek named after the spatula striking the wok; sate sellers use a high-pitched shout or a bell; mie ayam may use similar instruments — [Jakarta Post 2009](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2009/09/26/the-hills-are-alive-with-sound-noodles); [Jakarta Post 2022](https://www.thejakartapost.com/culture/2022/02/01/indonesian-icons-nasi-goreng-tek-tek-that-one-food-every-indonesian-loves.html); [Mojok](https://mojok.co/terminal/5-pedagang-makanan-keliling-yang-sering-wira-wiri-di-depan-rumah/); [Hipwee "10 professions you can guess by sound"](https://www.hipwee.com/hiburan/suara-abang-jualan-lewat/)
- Odong-odong: a pedal/motor kids' ride sold door to door, playing children's songs — [Republika](https://news.republika.co.id/berita/ob14wz408/mengenang-lagu-anakanak-lewat-odongodong)
- "Selamat Ulang Tahun" (Jamrud) written by Aziz MS in 2002; it is Jamrud's biggest royalty earner — [JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/music-movie/2508140281/selamat-ulang-tahun-jadi-lagu-jamrud-yang-paling-banyak-datangkan-royalti-begini-kata-sang-manager)
- Another "Selamat Ulang Tahun" was composed by Adikarso, recorded 1961 — [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2025/06/10/mengenal-adikarso-pencipta-lagu-papaja-cha-cha-dan-selamat-ulang-tahun)
- Indonesian copyright (UU 28/2014): songs protected for the author's life + 70 years — [detik](https://www.detik.com/pop/music/d-8050165/lagu-indonesia-raya-bebas-royalti); traditional songs/classical music cited as royalty-free categories — [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/entertainment/611027/4-jenis-musik-bebas-royalti-ada-musik-klasik-hingga-lagu-tradisional-indonesia)
- Remixing the azan as entertainment is considered an insult to religious symbols (NU view) — [NU Online](https://nu.or.id/nasional/azan-dijadikan-remix-adalah-penghinaan-simbol-agama-DdLXD)

### Inferences
- **New voice moods** (intro sound + effect + show on the gift):
  - **"Abang Bakso"**: intro "ting-ting-ting" bowl hits; voice slightly tinny/street echo; show: a steam puff + bakso balls bounce. Very recognisable and harmless.
  - **"Tek-tek"**: wok-spatula rhythm intro; show: sparks/flame flicker.
  - **"Odong-odong"**: tinny cheerful kids' tune intro (compose original; don't use copyrighted kids' songs), pitch-up wobble; show: gift bobs up and down.
  - **"Gamelan"** (slow, mystical, reverb + slight pitch-down, like a wayang dalang) — elegant, not mocking.
  - **"Angklung"**: shimmering bamboo arpeggio, tremolo effect on voice.
  - **"Keroncong"**: nostalgic old-radio filter (bandpass + crackle) with a cuk/ukulele strum intro — "radio jadul" vibe.
  - **"Kentongan / Ronda"**: night-watch bamboo knocks; "siskamling" echo. (Kentongan also signals danger in villages — keep playful rhythm, not alarm pattern.)
  - **"Koplo"**: kendang koplo beat intro — overlaps with existing Dangdut; maybe make Dangdut's intro koplo instead of adding a mood.
  - **"Sinetron"**: dramatic zoom "jeng-jeng-jeng!" stinger — huge meme recognition among 18–35 (uncited, my knowledge); voice with big reverb.
- **Music tunes (background)**: original short loops in gamelan pelog/slendro scales, angklung, keroncong, kendang koplo, gambang kromong (Betawi) — compose originals to avoid rights issues.
- **Birthday song**: do **not** use the Jamrud or Adikarso recordings/melodies. "Panjang umurnya" lyric: author unknown (gap) — safest to use only an instrumental of the public-domain "Happy Birthday" melody, or an original tune.
- **Takbiran / azan / sholawat / church hymns**: never as a mood or joke effect. Takbiran could at most be an ambient Lebaran backdrop sound, and better avoided entirely.

### Gaps
- Copyright status/author of the kids' "Panjang umurnya / serta mulia" lyric not found.
- Keroncong UNESCO status not found (no source).
- No source checked on the "sinetron jeng-jeng" meme.

## 4. Greetings/phrases (ASR value), stickers/emoji, batik decorations

### Takeaway
Common phrases mix Indonesian, English and abbreviations ("HBD", "WYATB"), which is exactly the ASR-hard material. Batik (UNESCO 2009) gives ready-made decoration patterns; kawung and megamendung are the safest, most drawable.

### Cited Findings
- "WYATB" = "Wish You All The Best", commonly attached to "HBD"; joke variant "HBD WYATB STNK KTP KTM SIM…" (pun on official document acronyms) — [GNFI](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/ragam/serba-serbi/ide-ucapan-ultah-wish-you-all-the-best); [Telkomsel](https://www.telkomsel.com/jelajah/jelajah-lifestyle/30-ucapan-ulang-tahun-lucu-dan-bikin-ngakak)
- Javanese birthday wishes exist as their own genre — [detikJogja](https://www.detik.com/jogja/berita/d-7091472/30-ucapan-selamat-ulang-tahun-bahasa-jawa-penuh-makna); short wishes for close friends — [detikJabar](https://www.detik.com/jabar/berita/d-6713132/30-contoh-ucapan-selamat-ulang-tahun-singkat-untuk-teman-dekat)
- Batik recognised by UNESCO on 2 Oct 2009 — [UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/ar/RL/-00170); [Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2009/09/08/batik-selected-unesco-cultural-heritage-list.html)
- Larangan motifs (e.g. Parang, Kawung) were reserved for the Yogyakarta palace (sultan's rule of 1927); parang still not worn by outsiders inside the palace; kawung = self-control/clean heart, now open to anyone; megamendung (Cirebon cloud motif) is not a larangan motif and reflects Chinese influence — [Humaniora journal (Binus)](https://journal.binus.ac.id/index.php/Humaniora/article/download/3260/2644/8629); [GNFI parang](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2023/09/19/motif-batik-parang); [GNFI 2024](https://www.goodnewsfromindonesia.id/2024/10/01/3-ragam-motif-batik-terpopoler-dan-makna-filosofisnya)

### Inferences
- **Phrases for prompts/demo scripts/ASR tests** (my compilation, common usage): "Selamat ulang tahun!", "HBD ya!", "WYATB", "Semoga panjang umur, sehat selalu, dan makin sukses", "Barakallah fii umrik" (Muslim birthday wish), "Selamat wisuda, S.Kom-nya!", "Akhirnya lulus juga!", "Selamat menempuh hidup baru", "Samawa (sakinah, mawaddah, warahmah)", "Mohon maaf lahir dan batin", "Minal aidin wal faizin", "Gong Xi Fa Cai", "Dirgahayu Republik Indonesia", "Merdeka!", slang: "gaskeun", "mantul", "anjay", "bestie", "wkwk", "cieee". Code-switching + Arabic loans + regional (Jawa "sugeng ambal warsa", Sunda "wilujeng tepang taun") are ASR stressors worth labelled data.
- **Sticker sets** (`decorate.stickers`, emoji): 🇮🇩 🍢(sate) 🍜(bakso) 🥥 🌶️ 🦎→(no crocodile-bread emoji; 🐊 for roti buaya) 🏮 🧧 🎋 🪁(layangan) 🛵(ojol/mudik) 🎓🧸 💐 💸 🍚 🕌(Lebaran: use sparingly) 🐓(kurungan) 🥁(bedug/kendang). Custom SVG stickers worth drawing: ketupat, krupuk, wayang silhouette (gunungan), ondel-ondel head, angklung, becak, layangan, odong-odong, gerobak bakso.
- **Patterns (`PATTERNS`)**: kawung (four ellipses in a grid — trivial SVG), megamendung (layered cloud curves, blue/red), parang (diagonal S-bands; fine as decoration but note palace origin), truntum (small star-flowers; traditionally worn by parents at Javanese weddings — uncited), Dayak/tenun stripes, ulos stripes, songket gold diamonds (Minang/Palembang).
- **Backdrops (`BACKDROPS`)**: rice terraces, Jakarta Monas at night, kampung street with gerobak lights, pelaminan Minang (gold-red), Bali penjor-lined road (respectfully), red-white umbul-umbul for Agustusan.

## 5. Sensitivity: what to avoid or handle carefully

### Takeaway
Religious sounds and symbols (azan, takbir, sholawat, Qur'an recitation, church/Hindu ritual items) must not be used as joke effects; Indonesia's blasphemy law is actively enforced and even complaints about azan volume have led to prison. The existing "Toa" mood is borderline because Toa evokes mosque loudspeakers — keep it as a generic announcement/megaphone (e.g. RT announcement, school PA) and never use azan/pengajian content in its intro.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Avoid entirely**: azan, takbir, sholawat, Qur'an recitation, church bells/hymns, Hindu mantras/kidung, Buddhist chanting as moods or comic intros; pork/alcohol imagery in stickers or food sets; dog stickers on Lebaran/Muslim-themed scenes; mocking ethnic accents (Batak, Madura, Papua, Chinese-Indonesian) as "moods" — ethnic accent voices are a common sinetron trope but risky.
- **Handle carefully**: penjor, canang sari, gunungan wayang, mosque/bedug, salib, Imlek deities — backdrop/decoration only, never interactive "throw/blow/cut" finales. Merah Putih: respectful use only. Real Rupiah banknote images: draw generic notes (Bank Indonesia rules on reproducing currency — not verified here).
- **Horror mood**: Indonesian horror icons (pocong, kuntilanak, tuyul, genderuwo) are hugely popular with 18–35 and fine as playful visuals (pocong hopping is a meme). Note pocong wears an Islamic burial shroud — some may find it tasteless at a wedding; keep to the Horror mood only. (Uncited, my judgment.)
- **Diversity**: provide parallel occasions (Lebaran, Natal, Imlek, Galungan greeting) rather than defaulting all "Indonesian" content to Javanese-Muslim symbols.
- Birthday wishes like "Barakallah fii umrik" are fine as text examples; don't auto-insert religious phrases.
