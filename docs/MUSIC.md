# Music source audit — 2026-11-19

`public/assets/music.js` contains four short note sequences (Party, Music box,
Waltz, Dreamy). Web Audio synthesizes their notes in the browser. It does not
load recordings, samples, streaming music, or a third-party music library.
`public/assets/voicefx.js` also synthesizes its mood sounds locally.

This establishes the implementation's sound source, not copyright clearance.
The repository does not document the melodies' authorship or a license.
No music fingerprinting/catalogue comparison was performed. Do not describe
these tunes as certified copyright-free or royalty-free on this basis.
Recreating a protected melody with synthesizers would still involve the
composition even without using someone else's recording.

For documented clearance, obtain the composer's provenance/permission or use
music with an explicit suitable license and keep the evidence here. “No music”
remains the default. This audit does not change the selected music on saved gifts.

Reference: US Copyright Office, Musical Compositions and Sound Recordings:
https://www.copyright.gov/register/pa-sr.html
